The details

Policies & information

Shipping & Delivery Policy +

D&T Corp (Pty) Ltd dispatches orders from our Johannesburg distribution hub Mon–Fri between 08:00 and 17:00 SAST.

  • Standard Courier (Consumer Electronics & Mini-UPS): R 149 flat rate nationwide; FREE on orders over R 2,500.
  • Gauteng Same/Next-Day Express: Orders placed before 11:00 SAST qualify for next-business-day delivery in Johannesburg and Pretoria.
  • Major Centres (Cape Town, Durban, Gqeberha, Bloemfontein): 2–3 working days.
  • Regional & Outlying Areas: 3–5 working days.
  • Heavy Freight (Hybrid Inverters & LiFePO4 Rack Batteries): Dispatched via insured palletized courier with tail-lift offload. A flat R 350 heavy-freight handling fee applies unless waived on qualifying bundle promotions.
Returns & Warranty Claims (CPA) +

All purchases are protected under the South African Consumer Protection Act 68 of 2008 (CPA) and the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act (ECTA).

  • 7-Day Cooling-Off Return (ECTA): Unopened, unused items in original factory-sealed packaging may be returned within 7 days of delivery.
  • 6-Month Implied CPA Warranty: Goods failing due to material or manufacturing defects within 6 months of delivery qualify for repair, replacement, or full refund at D&T Corp's expense.
  • Extended Manufacturer Warranty: Hybrid inverters carry a 2–5 year manufacturer warranty and LiFePO4 battery modules carry up to a 10-year / 6,000-cycle warranty when installed by a qualified Wireman's Licence electrician with a valid CoC.
  • How to Claim: Log a CPA warranty ticket directly in the Care tab with your Order Reference and product serial number.
POPIA Privacy Policy +

D&T Corp (Pty) Ltd (CIPC Reg No: 2026/000000/07) complies strictly with the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA).

  • Purpose of Collection: Contact numbers, delivery addresses, and order records are processed solely to fulfil orders, issue VAT tax invoices, arrange insured courier delivery, and administer CPA warranty claims.
  • Payment Security: Card, Instant EFT, and BNPL transactions via VISA, Mastercard, Payfast, Ozow EFT, Capitec Pay, and Payflex are processed through PCI-DSS Level 1 / SARB-compliant payment gateways. D&T Corp never stores full card numbers or banking PINs.
  • Your Rights: You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal records at any time by contacting our Information Officer at support@dtcorp.co.za.
